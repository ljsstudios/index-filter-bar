---
title: Custom layouts
description: "Use the bar's parts on their own."
---

`<IndexFilterBar>` is the bar's parts laid out as the admin's. For your own layout, use them directly; each takes its own `labels` group.

```tsx
import {
  ColumnsPopover,
  IndexBar,
  SaveControls,
  SearchField,
  ViewSwitcher,
  toSearchFieldLabels,
} from "@ljsstudios/index-filter-bar";

<IndexBar
  views={<ViewSwitcher popoverId="views" titles={titles} selected={0} onSelect={select} onAction={act} />}
  actions={<SaveControls idPrefix="products" dirty={dirty} onAll onReset={reset} onSaveAsNew={saveAs} onUpdate={update} />}
>
  <SearchField
    value={query}
    onChange={setQuery}
    fields={fields}
    filters={filters}
    onFiltersChange={setFilters}
    labels={toSearchFieldLabels()}
  />
</IndexBar>
```

A search box with filters and nothing else is just `SearchField`. See [components](/api/components) for each part's props.
